How this works
  • Lift = treated booking rate − holdout booking rate. Standard error = √(p₁(1−p₁)/n₁ + p₀(1−p₀)/n₀); the interval is lift ± 1.96 × SE.
  • Incremental appointments = lift × patients contacted. A cheap campaign with no proven lift is not a bargain.
  • Every figure is computed in dashkit.builders.outreach_effectiveness; the tests recompute the interval independently.
