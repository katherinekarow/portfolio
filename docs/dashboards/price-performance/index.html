Price or volume?

Sales growth splits into price change and case-volume change: (1 + price) × (1 + volume) − 1.

How this works
  • Season to date is weeks 1–26. Last season is cut at the same week so the comparison is like for like.
  • Average price is sales ÷ cases, so mix shifts between grades show up in the "All grades" view.
  • Data comes from dashkit.builders.price_performance: demand follows a seasonal curve and responds to price with a different elasticity per grade.
