How to read this

Target is the 60th-percentile pace of similar past sessions, and it never decreases. Historical range is the 10th to 90th percentile. Projection blends the session's own pickup with its segment's history, weighting history more heavily early in the appointment window, with a low/high band. Glide path is the route from today's appointments to the final target, shaped like appointments normally arrive. Outreach plan spreads the remaining gap over the weeks left: baseline follows the normal appointment shape, while milestones front-loads it (50% of the gap by a quarter of the remaining time, 80% by half). Sessions with a small gap or thin history are not eligible, so the plan doesn't over-allocate.
