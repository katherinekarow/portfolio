    How this works
    • Competitor listings arrive with inconsistent grade labels and lot references. They are matched to our lots with fuzzy grade mapping and an optimal one-to-one assignment, then deduplicated and screened for outliers (see the market-prices project).
    • Index = our price ÷ median competitor price for the same lot and grade on the same day. Cells need at least one valid competitor price.
    • A stale feed (the same prices repeated day after day) is flagged, not silently used.
