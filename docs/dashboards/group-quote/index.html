Group screening quote

An employer wants a block of slots at one clinic in one week. What is the lowest price that leaves the clinic no worse off than selling those slots to regular patients? All data is synthetic.

Floor vs list price by block size

The floor rises faster as the block grows: each extra slot given away is more likely to be one a regular patient would have booked. Hover for values.

How the floor is built

How this works
  • Regular demand for the remaining open slots is Poisson with the expected rate shown. Giving the k-th slot to the group loses the regular price × P(regular demand ≥ open − k + 1).
  • Floor = (displaced revenue + delivery cost per slot) × (1 + margin). List = regular price × slots, less a volume discount at 50 and 100 slots. The quote is whichever is higher.
  • The same formula runs in Python (dashkit.builders.quote), where a Monte Carlo simulation checks the displacement maths.
